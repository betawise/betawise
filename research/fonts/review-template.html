Click a glyph to flag or unflag it. Unicode aliases and an ASCII letter’s accented forms toggle together in the same face; Shift-click affects only that glyph. Accented glyphs can then be toggled individually. Flags are saved in this browser when available; export JSON for a portable copy. No font pixels are changed.
