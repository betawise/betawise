## BetaWise Terminal Medium (6×8)

The glyphs were independently recreated from the AlphaSmart 3000 LCD controller
font, including the custom modifications made by AlphaSmart. This recreation
is dedicated to the public domain as described above. It is not X11 Misc Fixed.

## BetaWise Terminal Small (5×7) and Tiny (4×6)

These sizes use public-domain X11 Misc Fixed artwork. The source font COPYRIGHT
property says:

> Public domain font.  Share and enjoy.

Sources: X.Org font-misc-misc, `5x7.pcf.gz` and `4x6.pcf.gz`.
[Upstream provenance and public-domain statement](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/ucs-fonts.html).
Printable ASCII was compared exactly against those sources. Our adaptations
are also dedicated to the public domain.
